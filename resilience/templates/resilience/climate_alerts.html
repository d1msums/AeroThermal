How this prototype assigns review priorities

Storage review: entered inflow exceeds available lake buffer. Reserve review: entered storage falls below the entered reserve. Other review flags come from unresolved reports, active internal advisories or a selected demo scenario. These are operational rules, not calibrated disaster thresholds.

Missing data stays unknown. Non-demo lake snapshots older than {{ freshness_hours }} hours cannot establish a current storage status. Demo snapshots remain usable for scenario exploration and are labelled. A report remains in the queue until staff resolve it.

Counts include all unresolved reports. The map shows up to the latest 500. Official national bulletins are not automatically matched to Elmina and never trigger discharge. Existing surface-temperature hotspots are not treated as measured heat exposure. El Niño cannot be inferred from a dry-vegetation report.
