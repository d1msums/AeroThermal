Data notes

Lake scenarios and storage history are demonstration inputs, not live telemetry. Hotspots are sample surface values, not imported thermal imagery.

Shade charts show saved geometric simulation results. They do not establish an air-temperature reduction, flood-safety score or energy saving.

App report examples use sample locations. Placeholder illustrations are labelled; actual stored photos appear when provided. B2C screens, app notifications and reward redemption belong to the mobile-app team.
